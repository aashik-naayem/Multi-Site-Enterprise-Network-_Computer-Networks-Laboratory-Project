## Site-to-Site IPsec VPN (HQ to Remote Branches)

Site-to-Site IPsec VPN tunnels are configured between the **HQ Central Router** and **four remote branch routers** to protect inter-site traffic with encryption and integrity verification rather than relying solely on Layer-3 routing.

### VPN Topology & Peer Mapping

```text
HQ Sales (192.168.10.0/24)
       |
 Central Router
       |
       +---- IPsec (5.1.1.1 ↔ 5.1.1.2) ----> Branch-1 (192.168.13.0/24)
       |
       +---- IPsec (6.1.1.1 ↔ 6.1.1.2) ----> Branch-2 (192.168.14.0/24)
       |
       +---- IPsec (7.1.1.1 ↔ 7.1.1.2) ----> Branch-3 (192.168.15.0/24)
       |
       +---- IPsec (8.1.1.1 ↔ 8.1.1.2) ----> Branch-4 (192.168.16.0/24)


 Tunnel,Local Peer (HQ),Remote Peer (Branch),Local Protected Subnet,Remote Protected Subnet
Branch-1 VPN,5.1.1.1,5.1.1.2,192.168.10.0/24 (Sales),192.168.13.0/24
Branch-2 VPN,6.1.1.1,6.1.1.2,192.168.10.0/24 (Sales),192.168.14.0/24
Branch-3 VPN,7.1.1.1,7.1.1.2,192.168.10.0/24 (Sales),192.168.15.0/24
Branch-4 VPN,8.1.1.1,8.1.1.2,192.168.10.0/24 (Sales),192.168.16.0/24

Verification & Live Proof Procedure
1. Verify IKE Phase 1 / Security Associations (QM_IDLE ACTIVE)
On the HQ Central Router, run:

Cisco CLI


show crypto isakmp sa
Expected Output: All four remote branch peers (5.1.1.2, 6.1.1.2, 7.1.1.2, 8.1.1.2) appear with state QM_IDLE and status ACTIVE, confirming that IKE/IPsec security associations are established.




2. Inspect Protected Subnets, Transform Set & Packet Counters
On the HQ Central Router, run:

Cisco CLI
show crypto ipsec sa
Protected Networks (local ident / remote ident): Confirms 192.168.10.0/255.255.255.0 protected against 192.168.13.0/255.255.255.0 (and .14.0, .15.0, .16.0 across the other tunnel sections) for the corresponding current_peer.

Encryption & Mode: Look for transform: esp-aes esp-sha-hmac, in use settings = {Tunnel, }, and Status: ACTIVE (ESP with AES encryption and SHA-HMAC integrity in Tunnel mode).

Baseline Counters: Note the initial #pkts encaps / #pkts encrypt and #pkts decaps / #pkts decrypt values (for example, encrypt: 18, decrypt: 16).

3. Live Traffic Encryption Test
Record baseline counters on the HQ Central Router using show crypto ipsec sa.

Generate traffic from the Sales PC (192.168.10.21) to the Branch-1 PC (192.168.13.21):

Plaintext
ping 192.168.13.21
Re-check counters immediately on the HQ Central Router:

Cisco CLI
show crypto ipsec sa
Expected Result: Both encrypt and decrypt counters increase (for example, from 18/16 to 22/20), proving packets are actively being encapsulated/encrypted outbound and decapsulated/decrypted inbound through the IPsec tunnel.

Verify all four branches: Ping reachable hosts in Branch-2 (192.168.14.x), Branch-3 (192.168.15.x), and Branch-4 (192.168.16.x) from the Sales PC, then confirm all four tunnels show QM_IDLE ACTIVE (show crypto isakmp sa), Status: ACTIVE, and non-zero/increasing encrypt and decrypt counters (show crypto ipsec sa).
